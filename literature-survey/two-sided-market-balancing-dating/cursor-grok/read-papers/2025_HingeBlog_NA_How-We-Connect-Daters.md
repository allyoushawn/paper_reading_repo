## Summary
Hinge’s 2025 Discover ranker is a deep-learning recommendation system that predicts who you might like **and** who is likely to like you. Dealbreakers are two-sided: you only see each other if both sides pass. Sending too many likes is framed as a signal problem (“we can’t tell what you truly want”). Paid unlimited dealbreakers are withheld because they would shrink everyone’s pools — an explicit **ecosystem** argument, not a CTR one. Newsroom: the mutual-compatibility rollout contributed to a **double-digit increase in matches overall**. No architecture, loss, or A/B protocol is published.
