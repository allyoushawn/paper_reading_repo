## Summary
If applications are cheap, applicants spray, employers cannot screen, employer welfare goes to zero. Caps (or costs) on applications restore screening and can Pareto-improve; both sides can get ≥3/4 of constrained-efficient welfare. Complements Kanoria & Saban (MS 2021) on *which side* may search.

## Project Relevance
**High.** Formal case for daily like limits as a market-clearing tool, not just a trust-and-safety knob. Pair with Fong 2024 on how caps should scale with market size.
