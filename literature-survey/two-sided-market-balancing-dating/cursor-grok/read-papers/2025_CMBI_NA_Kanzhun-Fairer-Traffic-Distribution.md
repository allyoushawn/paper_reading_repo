## Summary
BOSS Zhipin’s feed is recommendation-based rather than search-based. IR: “fairer traffic distribution.” CMBI/NLM: the model is “specifically designed to drive more traffic to users who are more responsive.” Matches require mutual consent to exchange resume/contact (“mutual achievement”). 2023: **4.8B** chat messages/month, **1.5B** mutual achievements. 88% of enterprises are SMEs; 66% of enterprise users are bosses. Nanbeige is a vertical LLM for job search/coaching, **not** the ranking architecture. No scoring formula or A/B %. Algorithm Ethics Working Team is named. This is LiJAR-style redistribution plus Kleinerman-style reply-weighting in product language.

## Project Relevance
**High analog** for Chinese two-sided recs (jobs, not dating). Closest public Chinese *traffic-allocation* sentence we have. Does **not** close 探探/陌陌/Soul ranking posts.
