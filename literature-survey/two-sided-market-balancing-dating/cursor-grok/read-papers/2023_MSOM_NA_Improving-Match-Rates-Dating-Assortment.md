## Summary
Daily profile assortments on a curated dating app. Identification: a partner algorithm change shows that recent matches *reduce* the probability of liking new profiles (users get pickier). Heuristics time high-match-probability profiles when the user is not already match-rich. Two field experiments at a major US dating company: **≥27% more matches** vs the partner’s algorithm (journal abstract / INFORMS). SSRN working paper also reports ~40% in simulations.

## Project Relevance
**High.** Only large dating *field* lift in this survey. Says the daily batch is a control problem with two-sided state (match stock), not a next-item CTR ranker.
