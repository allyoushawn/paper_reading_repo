## Summary
Field experiment on a major mobile dating app: pop-ups shift beliefs about local market size/competition. Thicker market → more selectivity. Structural counterfactuals: growing both sides 25% can *cut* matches 12% (men) / 17% (women) in small markets via extra pickiness; doubling the daily like limit reverses that.

## Project Relevance
**High.** Like limits are not just anti-spam; they are equilibrium instruments that must move with market thickness. Directly relevant to wasted likes vs starving users.
