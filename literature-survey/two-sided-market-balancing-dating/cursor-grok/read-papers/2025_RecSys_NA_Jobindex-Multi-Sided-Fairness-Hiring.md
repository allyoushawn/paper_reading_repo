# 2025 RecSys — Mapping Stakeholder Needs to Multi-Sided Fairness in Candidate Recommendation

**Title:** Mapping Stakeholder Needs to Multi-Sided Fairness in Candidate Recommendation for Algorithmic Hiring  
**Authors:** Mesut Kaya (Jobindex A/S), Toine Bogers (IT University of Copenhagen)  
**Year / venue:** 2025, RecSys  
**Link:** https://recsys.acm.org/recsys25/accepted-contributions/ (program; paper page via ACM)  
**Tier:** 1  
**nlm:** program page `bf49347b-e728-459a-8b9f-85ee20db9f6c`

## Summary
Jobindex candidate-to-recruiter recs. Interviews with **40** stakeholders (seekers, companies, recruiters, portal staff); co-design fairness definitions; map conflicting views onto existing fairness-metric categories. No architecture, no A/B, no dating. RecSys 2025 accepted-list mining: this is the hiring multi-stakeholder paper; Hayashi is the matching-market paper.

## Project Relevance
**Medium.** Confirms that “fairness” in two-sided hiring is multi-stakeholder, not seeker-only — same warning as Mehrotra CIKM 2018. Not a ranking lever.
