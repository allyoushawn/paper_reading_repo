## Summary
Match-maximizing reciprocal rankers still dump opportunity on popular users. Formalizes envy-freeness of *being recommended*. Alternating Nash social welfare (Frank–Wolfe) on expected utilities. On synthetic + real dating data: envy collapses (e.g. male envy 736→31 vs TU) while matches stay close (90 vs 103).

## Project Relevance
**High.** The fairness-of-opportunity layer on top of TU scoring. Survey 3 overlap — do not double-count as independent evidence.
