## 1. DESIGN PATTERNS

**1. Harmonic / personalized reciprocal fusion.** Sources: *Recommending People to People* (Pizzato et al., 2013); *Reciprocal Recommendation System for Online Dating* (Xia et al., 2015); *An Attempt to Personalize Preference Aggregation in Reciprocal Recommendation* (Ichimura, 2026). Mechanism: estimate both directional affinities and fuse with a (possibly user-specific) harmonic mean so a weak like-back suppresses the pair. Strongest numbers: RECON S@10 42.20% vs 23.00% unilateral; Wantedly unique recommendations@10 **+16.8%** vs fixed weights, with apply/match nDCG@10 **−2.4%/−3.3%**. Limitation: Xia reports no exact lift over RECON; Wantedly is offline and exposure-biased; no hard capacity.

**2. Dual-perspective / funnel reciprocal scoring.** Sources: *Modeling Two-Way Selection Preference for Person-Job Fit* (DPGNN); *Reciprocal Sequential Recommendation* (ReSeq); *BOSS*. Mechanism: separate outgoing taste from incoming appeal (graph nodes, sequential encoders, or MoE groups) and score the mutual outcome. Strongest numbers: ReSeq Technology HR@5 **0.7597/0.7809** vs DPGNN/FMLP-Rec **0.4521/0.5206**; BOSS live acceptance **+6.15%**. Limitation: recruitment, not dating; no capacity or match-spread objective.

**3. Transferable-utility market-clearing scores.** Sources: *Matching Theory-Based Recommender Systems in Online Dating* (Tomita, Togashi, Moriwaki 2022); CyberAgent blog *A Reciprocal Recommendation System Based on Matching Theory*; *Fast and Examination-agnostic Reciprocal Recommendation in Matching Markets* (Tomita et al. 2023). Mechanism: multiply reciprocal preference by equilibrium unmatched-probability / outside-option factors so superstars lose rank. Strongest numbers (2023 TU): synthetic n=100 expected matches **152.39** vs reciprocal product **129.82**; dating 1,000×1,000 **538.97/386.64** vs reciprocal **491.12/360.05**. Limitation: 2022 MTRS/blog report **no own quantitative outcomes**; 2023 is offline k-core, no live A/B.

**4. Exposure-budgeted / forecast-driven capacity allocation.** Sources: *Integrating Predictive Models into Two-Sided Recommendations* (ECDA); *LiJAR*; *Designing Recommendation Exposure and Favorite Lists* (TEC); *Recommendation with Capacity Constraints* (CapMF). Mechanism: cap expected inbound likes/dates, forecast oversubscription, or update stateful eligibility from remaining capacity. Strongest numbers: ECDA simulation effective dates **+7.6%**, receiver dating probability **+8.7%**, raw dates **−24.6%**; field (ex-top 0.1%) effective dates **+0.003** (p<0.05). LiJAR live: underserved apps **+6.5%**, overserved **−8.7%**, entropy **+12%**. TEC field: **+9.045** matches/prefecture-day. Limitation: LiJAR/TEC/CapMF are not double-opt-in dating; ECDA DID uses only two regions and messaging is unchanged.

**5. Constrained probabilistic / welfare reranking.** Sources: *Fairness of Exposure in Rankings*; *Joint Multisided Exposure Fairness*; *Fair Reciprocal Recommendation in Matching Markets* (NSW); *Two-Sided Fairness in Rankings via Lorenz Dominance*; *Fairness-Aware Ranking… LinkedIn Talent Search* (DetGreedy). Mechanism: LP / Frank–Wolfe / DetGreedy reallocates position exposure under fairness, envy, or Lorenz welfare. Strongest numbers: NSW dating 200×200 log-examination: matches **90.39** vs SW **111.37**, envy **31/14** vs **434/331**; DetGreedy representative queries **33%→95%**, InMails unchanged. Limitation: Singh–Joachims/JME/DetGreedy are passive-item or group-representation; NSW does not scale to full markets and has no live test.

**6. Market-design levers (caps, signals, menus, who searches).** Sources: *Propose with a Rose?*; *Effects of Market Size and Competition… Online Dating* (Fong); *Managing Congestion in Matching Markets* (Arnosti et al.); *Facilitating the Search for Partners*; *Competing by Restricting Choice*; *Assortment Planning for Two-Sided Sequential Matching Markets*. Mechanism: scarce roses, like limits, application caps, directional initiation, menu-size restriction, collision-aware assortments. Strongest numbers: roses, acceptance **+3.3 pp** (~20%; IV **+4.1 pp**), eight vs two roses initiated dates **+48%** verified Seoul men / **+86%** women; Fong small-market 25% growth **−12.2%/−17.7%** matches, doubled like limits **+136.3%/+121.6%**. Limitation: Arnosti/Kanoria–Saban/Halaburda/Ashlagi are theory/simulation; roses waste ~30% of signals on non-responding top-tier recipients.

**7. Interference-aware evaluation of two-sided policies.** Sources: *A/B Testing for Recommender Systems in a Two-Sided Marketplace* (UniCoRn); *Experimental Design in Two-Sided Platforms* (TSRI); *Multiple Randomization Designs*; *Reducing Interference Bias… Airbnb* (GCR); *Reducing Marketplace Interference Bias Via Shadow Prices*; *Off-Policy Evaluation and Learning for Matching Markets* (DiPS/DPR); Kuaishou causal-design recap. Mechanism: producer-side rank blending, two-sided cells, cluster randomization, shadow prices, two-stage OPE. Strongest numbers: UniCoRn LinkedIn WAU **+0.51%**/sessions **+0.57%**; GCR **19.76%** of naive booking effect is interference; DPR lowest MSE on Wantedly 1.2% matches. Limitation: SP requires centralized LP matching (dating fit **Low**); Kuaishou reports **no numbers**.

**8. Ecosystem diagnostics without an allocator.** Sources: *Your Looks and Your Inbox*; *Aspirational Pursuit of Mates*; *Matching and Sorting in Online Dating*; Tapple *Analyzing Encounters on Matching Apps*. Mechanism: attractiveness-stratum message shares, PageRank desirability gaps, unrequited-contact rates, side-specific recall. Strongest numbers: OkCupid two-thirds of male messages to top third of women; Bruch–Newman men/women message **26%/23%** upward, men’s upward reply **≤21%**; Hitsch 71%/56% first contacts unrequited; Tapple recall **0.9/0.2**. Limitation: observational; no tested reallocation.

## 2. SIX-ROW MATRIX EVIDENCE

### reciprocal scoring
- **RECON (Pizzato et al. 2013):** harmonic bilateral compatibility; S@10 42.20% vs 23.00% unilateral.
- **DPGNN (Yang et al. 2022):** dual active/passive nodes; Tech Recall@5 0.2941/0.3430 vs LGCNBERT 0.2685/0.3187.
- **ReSeq (Zheng et al. 2023):** sequential dual encoders; Technology HR@5 0.7597/0.7809.
- **CRRS (Yang et al. 2024):** causal one-sided vs bilateral treatments; Libimseti CRecall@50 0.3387 and 1,743 pairs vs DPGNN 0.3007 / 1,548.
- **Ichimura 2026:** personalized harmonic β; unique@10 +16.8%, nDCG down.
- **TinVec / Tinder 2019 / Hinge 2025:** unilateral or qualitative reciprocal production scoring; TinVec AUROC 90% / F1 85%; Tinder and Hinge effects **Not specified**.

### capacity-aware scoring
- **TU 2023:** IPFP outside-option factors in a MIPS-compatible score; dating 1,000×1,000 538.97/386.64 expected matches vs reciprocal 491.12/360.05.
- **MTRS 2022:** `sqrt(μ_x0)·sqrt(μ_y0)` capacity discount; **no quantitative effect specified**.
- **ECDA 2026:** date-rate greedy allocation under expected-like/date caps; sim effective dates +7.6%, raw dates −24.6%.
- **LiJAR 2017:** forecast min/max apps then boost/penalize; +6.5%/−8.7% applications, entropy +12%.
- **CapMF 2017:** expected-usage penalty; MovieLens Cap-BPR capacity loss 4.51→0.08.
- **TEC 2026:** capacity/vacancy score updates; field +9.045 matches/prefecture-day.

### constrained re-ranking
- **Singh & Joachims 2018:** LP + Birkhoff–von Neumann; job DTR 1.7483→1.0000, DCG 3.8193→3.8044.
- **JME 2022:** GG-F + stochastic ranking; MovieLens100K NDCG@50 0.3703→0.3692, GG-F improved p<0.01.
- **NSW 2024:** alternating Nash welfare; 90.39 matches and 31/14 envies vs SW 111.37 and 434/331.
- **LorenzWelfare 2021:** concave reciprocal welfare; Twitter-13k bottom-10% utility 120→280, total 17,000→6,400.
- **DetGreedy 2019:** prefix representation; queries 33%→95%, InMails unchanged.
- **CRRS vacant-slot rerank:** redirects redundant bilateral slots; unique-pair lift as above.
- **Airbnb Query-Context Embedding 2020:** listwise diversification; bookings +0.44%.

### market-design lever
- **Lee & Niederle 2015:** scarce roses; +3.3 pp acceptance; 8 vs 2 roses +48%/+86% initiated dates.
- **Fong 2024:** like-limit × thickness; doubled caps reverse growth losses to +136.3%/+121.6% matches.
- **Arnosti, Johari, Kanoria 2021:** application caps; welfare ~2× at r=1.4, 3× at r=1.9; ≥75% of constrained-efficient maxima.
- **Kanoria & Saban 2021:** who may propose; up to +14.6% average welfare; 2:1 imbalance long side +31%.
- **Halaburda et al. 2018:** restrict N; rejection N/(N+1); utility peaks near N=2–3 in simulations.
- **Hinge 2025:** gate unlimited dealbreakers to protect pool thickness; effect **Not specified**.
- **Ashlagi et al. 2022:** collision-aware menus; ALG/UB 0.37–0.47.

### ecosystem metrics
- **OkCupid 2009:** message concentration; 66% of male messages to top 33% of women; top women ~5×/28×.
- **Bruch & Newman 2018:** PageRank gap; 26%/23% upward targeting; men’s upward reply ≤21%.
- **Hitsch et al. 2010:** 71%/56% unrequited first contacts; 4.3%/6.4% convert to matches.
- **NSW 2024:** expected matches and envy counts (card); Gini **Not specified on the NSW card**.
- **ECDA 2026:** average dates, effective dates, dating probability, likes; messaging unchanged in field.
- **Tapple 2022 (Kazumi):** recall 0.9/0.2; verification message-approval +2% / ~+36%.
- **MRet 2026:** two-sided retention as objective; **no numeric retention effect specified**.
- **CRRS 2024:** CRecall/CPrecision, SRecall, RNDCG, unique true-positive pairs.

### evaluation method
- **UniCoRn 2021:** producer-side counterfactual rank blending; LinkedIn WAU +0.51%, p<0.001.
- **Johari et al. 2022 (TSRI-2):** two-sided randomization + cannibalization correction; balanced-demand |bias| ~0.08 vs 0.20–0.22.
- **Masoero et al. 2021 (SMRD):** four pair-cells; single-sided profit sign wrong; spillover test 99.5%.

- **Kuaishou 2021:** bilateral + switchback designs; effects **Not specified**.
