# Paper Analysis: ESCM2: Entire Space Counterfactual Multi-Task Model for Post-Click Conversion Rate Estimation

**Source:** `/Users/fox/Projects/paper_reading_repo/literature-survey/unified-ltv-ranking-dating/claude_opus/pdfs/2204.05125.pdf`
**Date analyzed:** 2026-08-17

## 1. Summary

**Title:** ESCM2: Entire Space Counterfactual Multi-Task Model for Post-Click Conversion Rate Estimation
**Authors:** Hao Wang, Tai-Wei Chang, Tianqiao Liu, Jianmin Huang, Zhichao Chen, Chao Yu, Ruopeng Li, Wei Chu (Ant Group)
**Venue/Year:** SIGIR 2022

**Abstract (paraphrased):** Entire Space Multi-task Model (ESMM) and its family address CVR estimation's data-sparsity problem by leveraging the impression → click → conversion sequential pattern, but still fail to guarantee unbiasedness of the CVR estimate. The paper theoretically demonstrates two problems in ESMM — Inherent Estimation Bias (IEB) and Potential Independence Priority (PIP) — and proposes ESCM2, which adds a counterfactual risk minimizer as a regularizer on ESMM to address both simultaneously. Offline and online experiments show ESCM2 mitigates IEB/PIP and outperforms baselines.

**Key contributions:**
1. First work (per the authors) to rigorously prove, via Theorem 1, that ESMM's CVR estimate is inherently biased — always strictly larger than the ground truth over the exposure space (`Bias^ESMM > 0`) — with supporting experiments, not just prior empirical suspicion (cited to Zhang et al. [27], i.e., the Multi-IPW/Multi-DR WWW 2020 paper already in this survey).
2. Identifies a second, distinct flaw — Potential Independence Priority (PIP) — showing ESMM's CTCVR estimation is prone to conditional independence between click and conversion, i.e., ESMM's causal graph omits the O→R edge and so implicitly models `P(r=1)` instead of the causally correct `P(r=1|do(o=1))`.
3. Proposes ESCM2, the first work (per the authors) to improve ESMM from a causal-inference perspective: a counterfactual risk minimizer (CRM), instantiated as either an inverse-propensity-score (ESCM2-IPS) or doubly-robust (ESCM2-DR) regularizer, added on top of ESMM's existing CTR + CTCVR losses.
4. Extensive offline (Ali-CCP + Ant Insurance industrial dataset) and **online A/B** experiments (three production scenarios at Ant Insurance) demonstrating IEB/PIP mitigation and business-metric gains.

**Methodology:** ESCM2 keeps ESMM's two-tower CTR/CVR backbone (shared embedding lookup table, MMoE feature extractor) and adds a third learning objective. The full loss is `L_ESCM2 = L_CTR + λc·L_CVR + λg·L_CTCVR`, where `L_CTR` is the standard empirical risk over the entire exposure space, `L_CTCVR` is ESMM's global risk over predicted CTR × predicted CVR, and `L_CVR` is now a **counterfactual risk** rather than ESMM's naive click-space risk. ESCM2-IPS computes this counterfactual risk by inversely weighting each pair's CVR loss by the CTR tower's own output used as a propensity score (Eq. 15), proven (Theorem 2) to converge to the ideal loss `P` given accurate propensities. ESCM2-DR augments this with a third, jointly-trained imputation tower estimating the CVR prediction error, giving unbiasedness if *either* the propensity score *or* the imputation model is accurate. Both regularizers are shown (Theorem 3, via do-calculus intervention on the click node O) to make the CVR tower converge toward `P(r=1|do(o=1))`, the causally correct counterfactual conversion rate, simultaneously eliminating IEB and PIP.

**Main results:** On Ali-CCP and a large Ant Insurance industrial dataset (37.73M users, 61.58M training exposures), ESCM2-IPS/DR beat ESMM and several IPW/DR/imputation-based multi-task baselines (Naïve, MTL-IMP, MTL-EIB, MTL-IPS, MTL-DR) on CVR-AUC/KS/F1/Recall and CTCVR-AUC/KS/F1/Recall. Table 5 shows ESMM's CVR estimate overshoots the ground-truth conversion label expectation (e.g., Ali-CCP test set: ESMM 0.0057 above ground truth vs. much smaller gaps for ESCM2-IPS/DR), directly evidencing Theorem 1's IEB claim. Three online A/B (switchback) experiments at Ant Insurance's real production insurance-recommendation scenarios showed ESCM2 improving order quantity by up to 40.55% and premium by up to 12.69% over ESMM in one scenario (Wufu campaign), with consistent UV-CVR/UV-CTCVR gains in the other two.

## 2. Experiment Critique

- **Design:** The paper's strongest methodological asset is that its offline claims are backed by formal theorems (Theorem 1: ESMM's IEB; Theorems 2–3: ESCM2-IPS's unbiasedness and causal correctness) rather than only empirical comparison — directly mirroring, and explicitly extending, the causal-proof style of Zhang et al.'s WWW 2020 Multi-IPW/Multi-DR paper already catalogued in this survey (`2020_WWW_MultiDR_Causal-Debiasing-Post-Click-CVR-Multi-task.md`).
- **Statistical validity:** Offline CVR/CTCVR tables (Tables 2–3) report mean ± std over ten runs with paired-samples t-tests (p < 0.01) against the best baseline — solid statistical practice for the offline comparison.
- **Online experiments:** Three live A/B (switchback, not user-level A/B, due to shared recommendation pool) tests are reported at Ant Insurance across 4–6 days each, covering millions of unique visitors — genuinely rare among the papers in this survey to have real online business-metric results (order quantity, premium, UV-CVR, UV-CTCVR) alongside the offline theory. Table 4's daily breakdown, however, shows substantial day-to-day noise (e.g., Day 4 order count −12.49% before rebounding), which the paper reports honestly but does not statistically test.
- **Reproducibility:** Ali-CCP is public and standard; the Ant Insurance industrial dataset is proprietary and, per the paper's own footnote, **"the data set was destroyed after the experiment"** — a hard reproducibility ceiling on the industrial-scale claims.
- **DR proof gap (self-acknowledged shortcut):** The paper explicitly states, for the DR regularizer's IEB/PIP mitigation, "We omit the exact proof for brevity" (Section 4.1.2) — a real, stated gap; the DR variant's theoretical guarantee is asserted by analogy to the IPS proof, not independently derived in the paper.
- **Stated limitation:** The authors themselves note ESCM2's fragility on more complex sequential user-behavior patterns (beyond the simple impression→click→conversion chain) as future work, and flag that IPS/DR unbiasedness depends on the CTR tower's propensity estimate being accurate — an assumption they call fragile ("propensity score's fragility," Section 7).

## 3. Industry Contribution

- **Deployability:** Validated at real Ant Group production scale (both a large industrial dataset and — unusually for this survey — genuine online A/B/switchback deployment across three live business scenarios), with an internal C++ deep learning framework used for the online experiments.
- **Problems solved:** Targets the same industrial pain point as ESMM (selection bias from training CVR only on clicked data) but as a **drop-in regularizer** rather than a full architectural replacement — ESCM2 is explicitly framed as augmenting ESMM, keeping the same CTR/CVR two-tower backbone and MMoE feature extractor, which lowers the engineering cost of adoption relative to a from-scratch redesign.
- **Engineering cost:** Low incremental cost — implementation guidance (Appendix A) reduces to four steps: build a standard ESMM, compute a propensity score (with clipping, e.g. threshold 0.1, to control variance), compute the counterfactual CVR loss with truncated propensity gradients, and tune the counterfactual/global risk weights (λc, λg) via a small grid (the paper finds λc ∈ [0, 0.1] and λg ∈ [0, 3] work well, per the parameter-sensitivity study in Section 5.5).
- **Ranking pipeline framing:** Same architectural slot as ESMM in a CTR→CVR ranking pipeline (Figure 1: candidate items → multi-task ranking model → weighted-sum ranking); not a full ranking/serving model itself but a training-time correction for one component (the CVR/CTCVR estimate) of such a pipeline.

## 4. Novelty vs. Prior Work

Novelty is framed primarily against three prior lines: (a) ESMM (Ma et al., 2018) — the paper's central target, for which it supplies the first rigorous proof (per the authors) that ESMM is biased (IEB) *and* subject to a distinct causal-graph flaw (PIP) that prior empirical work (Zhang et al. 2020, cited as [27]) had not framed in causal-graph terms; (b) Zhang et al.'s WWW 2020 Multi-IPW/Multi-DR paper, whose IPS/DR theorem the authors explicitly "extend" (Theorem 2's proof directly builds on and cites [27]) to show the IPS-based counterfactual regularizer is equivalent to the ideal loss `P`; (c) other entire-space or MNAR-debiasing methods surveyed in Related Work (ESM2, GMCM, HM3, and Schnabel et al.'s unbiased learning-to-rank), none of which the paper claims combine a causal do-calculus framing with a regularizer that is a minimal, drop-in addition to an existing ESMM-style production pipeline. Structurally, ESCM2's three-branch architecture (empirical risk / counterfactual risk / global risk, Figure 4) closely parallels Multi-IPW/Multi-DR's three-task chain (CTR / CVR / Imputation) — the two papers converge on very similar IPS/DR machinery; ESCM2's differentiator is the causal-graph (PIP) argument and its own do-calculus derivation, plus a from-existing-ESMM integration framing and genuine online validation, rather than a fundamentally different debiasing mechanism.

## Project Relevance

Speaks directly to **Q2** (prediction-vs-incrementality distinction) and **Q1/Q6** in a narrower sense (it is not an LTV/retention objective, but it is a rigorous example of correcting a biased short-term-event estimator the project's own like/match/conversation predictor structurally resembles, and it is one of the few papers in this survey with genuine **online** A/B evidence, relevant to Q6's evaluation-under-real-deployment question). As the batch note flags, this is Alibaba-lineage's later (and Ant Group's) answer to the same ESMM-bias problem the WWW 2020 Multi-IPW/Multi-DR paper (`2020_WWW_MultiDR_Causal-Debiasing-Post-Click-CVR-Multi-task.md`) already in this KB addresses: ESCM2 proves the identical qualitative result (ESMM systematically overestimates CVR, Theorem 1) via a closely related IPS/DR causal-regularizer mechanism, but packages it as a **minimal add-on regularizer to an existing ESMM pipeline** rather than a restructured multi-task architecture, and — critically for de-risking the project's own migration — backs the offline theory with **real online business-metric lift**. For the project's entire-space modeling decision, ESCM2 is the more integration-friendly variant to prototype first (lower engineering delta from a likely-already-ESMM-shaped like/match/conversation predictor), while the WWW 2020 paper offers the more foundational/general causal-estimator toolkit if a fuller rebuild is later justified. It does not address Q3 (no delayed label beyond the immediate click/conversion event), Q4 (no head-fusion beyond the CTR-as-propensity mechanism), Q5 (no incrementality of exposure at all — the same gap the batch note flags for the WWW 2020 paper applies here identically), Q7 (no two-sided/reciprocal/congestion/fairness treatment), or Q8 (no staged-migration guidance beyond "add this regularizer to your existing ESMM").
