**Summary.** Standard recommender policies (contextual bandits) assume static viewer and provider populations. ### CanniUplift: A Holistic Framework for Mitigating Seller and Incentive Cannibalization in E-commerce Uplift Modeling

**Summary.** Standard uplift models (DragonNet, EUEN, etc.) implicitly assume SUTVA (no interference between units), which is violated in multi-seller e-commerce by two "cannibalization" effects: (1) Seller-level cannibalization — an incentive to seller A diverts a user's organic spend from seller B, so individual-shop uplift is a zero-sum reallocation that doesn't grow platform GMV; (2) Incentive-level cannibalization — users with strong organic intent or better concurrent coupons convert without redeeming the assigned coupon ("conversion without redemption"), so the model misattributes organic demand as treatment effect. CanniUplift addresses both with: Platform-level Global Alignment (PGA) — a multi-head architecture that aggregates per-seller GMV predictions per user and constrains their sum to match observed total platform GMV, penalizing models that predict spurious uplift for multiple shops simultaneously (an internal-consistency/anti-double-counting constraint); Redemption-based Decomposition Denoising (RDD) — factorizes the treated outcome into a redemption path (r=1) and non-redemption path (r=0) within an entire-space framework, so conversions without redemption are modeled separately and don't inflate uplift estimates; and a Treat-Attention mechanism (candidate as query, multi-source user behavior/treatment sequence as key/value) for fine-grained user-candidate heterogeneity.

**Evidence.** - Industrial dataset (Taobao/Tmall production logs): baseline models overestimate aggregated individual treatment effects by ~35% relative to observed online effect; ~25% of treated conversions fail to redeem the assigned coupon (pseudo-conversion noise).
- Offline (industrial dataset): CanniUplift achieves Seller AUUC 0.769 / QINI 0.314 and User(platform) AUUC 0.849 / QINI 0.348 vs. best baseline EUEN's 0.744/0.297 and 0.794/0.319 respectively; ablation shows PGA alone raises User AUUC to 0.826, RDD alone to 0.818.

- RDD reduces uplift overestimation among non-redemption (r=0) conversions by 7.6% (3.192 vs. 3.435 predicted uplift).
- Live production A/B test (Taobao/Tmall, Oct 5-12, 2025): Marketing cost -2.45%, Platform incremental GMV +4.08%, Platform ROI +6.69%.
