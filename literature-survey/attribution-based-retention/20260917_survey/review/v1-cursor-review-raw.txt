## C. ANSWER QUALITY

**Q1.** The useful pattern is incrementality-over-response plus some experiment or identification layer (Li, Wei, Snap HTE, DoorDash DML, Instagram). Calling it “granular observational credit calibrated by RCTs” is too tight for Li (not granular) and too loose for Wu/Bhat/ALM-MTA (not that stack). Name the pattern as: observational scores, if used, are not the objective; decisions are trained or calibrated on incremental outcomes. Put Li/Wei/CanniUplift/Snap in the answer; move LLM/MMM to landscape.

**Q2.** The ranking-vs-attribution split is correct for MRet and Wantedly (`relevance-index-3.md` ~L41, ~L74). Direct credit is correctly rare; IURO and GFN belong. Pinterest Downstream Rewards does not belong in that sentence without “partly.” Negative scope: no named dating-company paper is right (D3 ~L7; MRet is Hanjuku-kaso on unnamed data). Do not extend that negative to Tencent, and do not treat D3’s “direct” tags on MRet/Wantedly/Impatient Bandits as ground truth — the cards override them.

## D. RECOMMENDATION

v1 formula is well-defined as a predictive deletion residual: inputs `H`, `e_j`, binary `Y_N7`; output theoretically in [−1,1]; signed vs `c_j^+` is specified. It is not an incremental probability. No v1-cited paper estimates that exact `do()` contrast on N7. ALM-MTA deletion is front-door upload credit; SMEO masking is conversion prefixes; IURO is attention scores.

Selection-bias device is a collage. Downstream Rewards used UP-normalization on proxy features, not on deletion credits (`cards-summaries-4.md` ~L15). Propensity/position are Wei/SMEO/Taobao, not in the v1 source list. No cited v1 paper used this bundle.

Validation anchor: a new exposure/score-perturbation holdout is realistic (Instagram 50/50; Snap ITT) but does not exist yet. v1 cannot inherit Li-style calibration without it. Two-sided interference is named only in v2; it is a v1 risk (CRRS bilateral treatment, `cards-summaries-1.md` ~L89). Other missing risks: leave-one-out effects need not sum to `Y`; ranker can fit user propensity rather than swipe quality; match as later feature leaks policy.

**Do not ship v1 as written.** Ship as an explanation/debug score, or wait for an independent holdout and a conservation constraint, and stop calling `c_j` incremental.

## E. IS THE MEASUREMENT RIGGED

Partly, until the holdout exists. Deletion labels from a model of `P(Y_N7|H)` are highly correlated with user activity; a downstream ranker trained on those labels can look strong on user-level N7 / log loss without discriminating causal swipes. AUUC/Qini “on randomized traffic” is not identified on observational last-touch-like data. Conservation vs “user-level predicted N7 lift” can be satisfied by construction from the same `P(Y|H)`.

Present: forward splits; proposed negative-control outcomes; placebo timestamps; don’t recycle the calibration cohort. Missing: **placebo touch** (credit a never-shown profile / post-cutoff swipe); a treatment assigned independently of the attributor; specified negative controls (not just the phrase). Experiment-level conservation, if the holdout is real and frozen, is the hard-to-game piece.
