# CanniUplift: A Holistic Framework for Mitigating Seller and Incentive Cannibalization in E-commerce Uplift Modeling

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.05242.pdf | **NLM source id:** acc8a491-158c-4f24-b43a-833a622ec5d5 | **Year / venue:** KDD '26 (32nd ACM SIGKDD), arXiv 2607.05242, Jeju Island, Korea, Aug 2026 | **Authors / affiliation:** Zuwang He, Shihao Shu, Yuli Qu (co-first authors), Hanyu Gao, Ziliang Zhang, Diwei Chen, Xiangda Yan, Buyu Gao, Tanchao Zhu, Yumeng Li (corresponding), Junxiong Zhu — all Taobao & Tmall Group of Alibaba | **Analyzed:** 2026-09-17 (NotebookLM, 2 queries)

## 1. Summary
Standard uplift models (DragonNet, EUEN, etc.) implicitly assume SUTVA (no interference between units), which is violated in multi-seller e-commerce by two "cannibalization" effects: (1) Seller-level cannibalization — an incentive to seller A diverts a user's organic spend from seller B, so individual-shop uplift is a zero-sum reallocation that doesn't grow platform GMV; (2) Incentive-level cannibalization — users with strong organic intent or better concurrent coupons convert without redeeming the assigned coupon ("conversion without redemption"), so the model misattributes organic demand as treatment effect. CanniUplift addresses both with: Platform-level Global Alignment (PGA) — a multi-head architecture that aggregates per-seller GMV predictions per user and constrains their sum to match observed total platform GMV, penalizing models that predict spurious uplift for multiple shops simultaneously (an internal-consistency/anti-double-counting constraint); Redemption-based Decomposition Denoising (RDD) — factorizes the treated outcome into a redemption path (r=1) and non-redemption path (r=0) within an entire-space framework, so conversions without redemption are modeled separately and don't inflate uplift estimates; and a Treat-Attention mechanism (candidate as query, multi-source user behavior/treatment sequence as key/value) for fine-grained user-candidate heterogeneity.
**Unit of credit:** per user-candidate seller (or user-candidate incentive) pair. **Outcome:** platform-wide net incremental GMV and ROI (not retention). **Bias handling:** PGA corrects for cross-unit interference/zero-sum substitution (a SUTVA violation, conceptually adjacent to but distinct from selection bias); RDD corrects for "would have converted anyway" pseudo-incrementality by conditioning on an observed post-treatment redemption signal, directly analogous to "credits swipes that would have happened anyway."

## 2. Evidence
- Industrial dataset (Taobao/Tmall production logs): baseline models overestimate aggregated individual treatment effects by ~35% relative to observed online effect; ~25% of treated conversions fail to redeem the assigned coupon (pseudo-conversion noise).
- Offline (industrial dataset): CanniUplift achieves Seller AUUC 0.769 / QINI 0.314 and User(platform) AUUC 0.849 / QINI 0.348 vs. best baseline EUEN's 0.744/0.297 and 0.794/0.319 respectively; ablation shows PGA alone raises User AUUC to 0.826, RDD alone to 0.818.
- Synthetic dataset (20,000 users, 500,000 sellers, 50 assigned/user, 10 semantic segments with exponential uplift-decay cannibalization rules): CanniUplift 1.1596 Seller AUUC / 1.1474 User AUUC vs. EUEN 1.0940/1.1245.
- RDD reduces uplift overestimation among non-redemption (r=0) conversions by 7.6% (3.192 vs. 3.435 predicted uplift).
- Live production A/B test (Taobao/Tmall, Oct 5-12, 2025): Marketing cost -2.45%, Platform incremental GMV +4.08%, Platform ROI +6.69%.

## 3. Limitations
- Does not model temporal cannibalization — short-term promotions may pull forward future purchases, inflating short-term uplift at the expense of long-term revenue (directly analogous to a dating-platform concern: a swipe boost today borrowing against future engagement).
- Relies on historical behavior sequences to estimate cannibalization effects, so it suffers latency capturing real-time substitution patterns.
- At inference time, uses a single-point counterfactual approximation rather than exhaustively recomputing counterfactual states across all competing sellers, for serving-latency reasons.

## 4. Prior works named
- Rubin (1974) / Holland (1986) — Neyman-Rubin potential outcomes framework and SUTVA
- Ma et al. (2018) — ESMM, Entire Space Multi-Task Model (basis for the entire-space RDD design)
- Ke et al. (2021) — EUEN, addressing exposure bias in uplift modeling (primary baseline)
- Shi, Blei & Veitch (2019) DragonNet; Shalit, Johansson & Sontag (2017) CFRNet — representation-learning uplift baselines
- Ma et al. (2018) MMoE; Tang et al. (2020) PLE — multi-task learning architectures informing the multi-head design

## 5. Project Relevance
- **Answers:** Q1 (2026 industrial uplift-modeling system, Alibaba/Taobao production, directly targets the "would have converted anyway" selection-bias problem).
- **Attributes retention to individual interactions?** No — estimates incremental GMV from personalized incentive allocation to sellers/coupons; not a retention or multi-touch attribution method, though its debiasing logic is highly analogous.
- **Transferable components:** RDD is the most directly relevant idea in this batch to "credits swipes that would have happened anyway" — it explicitly separates a post-treatment observable signal (redemption vs. non-redemption) to strip out organic/pseudo-incrementality, which maps onto separating "swipe led to a genuine match/conversation" from "swipe/match that would have retained the user regardless"; PGA's platform-wide aggregation-consistency constraint maps onto ensuring per-swipe fractional credits sum consistently to the true observed N7 outcome per user rather than each candidate profile independently over-claiming credit (an internal-consistency check analogous to fractional-credit normalization); the Treat-Attention mechanism (candidate as query over a user's behavior/treatment sequence) is a directly reusable architecture pattern for scoring each candidate swipe's incremental contribution against a user's history.
- **Required changes:** Retarget outcome from platform GMV to daily N7-active retention; redefine "redemption" analogously as a genuine downstream signal (e.g., match converts to a real conversation) vs. a swipe/match with no downstream engagement; extend to a full heterogeneous event-type sequence rather than seller/coupon candidates.
- **On last-touch:** Not discussed directly by name, but the paper's core critique — that naive local (per-unit) uplift models over-attribute credit to organic conversions and ignore cross-unit interference, overestimating true incrementality by ~35% — is structurally the same failure mode as last-touch attribution over-crediting swipes that would have happened anyway.
- **Transfer rating:** adaptable — RDD's redemption-based denoising and PGA's aggregation-consistency constraint are concrete, directly portable mechanisms for exactly the selection-bias and double-crediting problems the dating platform's retention-attribution project is trying to solve.
