# Update on Plans for Privacy Sandbox Technologies (Attribution Reporting API retired)

**Source:** https://privacysandbox.google.com/blog/update-on-plans-for-privacy-sandbox-technologies | **Retrieved via:** NotebookLM source da122d2b-c07a-4520-93b1-90e48a2a77aa | **Year / venue:** 2025-10, Google Blog | **Authors / team:** Anthony Chavez (VP, Privacy Sandbox, Google) | **Analyzed:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Summary
Google announces retirement of several Privacy Sandbox APIs (Attribution Reporting API, Protected Audience, Topics, Private Aggregation, IP Protection, On-Device Personalization, and others) due to low ecosystem adoption. Google will keep supporting CHIPS/FedCM and collaborate with the W3C Private Advertising Technology Working Group (PATWG) on an open, interoperable web attribution standard. This is a policy/product announcement about browser-level ad-measurement infrastructure, not an attribution methodology or algorithm.

## 2. Evidence
Qualitative only: cites ecosystem/developer feedback and observed low adoption of the retired APIs. No quantitative data, experiment results, or numbers are reported.

## 3. Limitations
Covers only macro industry direction, not a technical method. Acknowledges browser-API-level attribution alone could not meet ecosystem needs, motivating a shift to collaborative open standards.
