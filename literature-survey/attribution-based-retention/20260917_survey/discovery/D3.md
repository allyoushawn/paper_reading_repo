## Notes

- Confirmed near-duplicate: the ByteDance/Douyin "session-level return-time-percentile reward" description found under row 3 is the already-known Kuaishou RLUR (WWW 2023, arXiv 2302.01724) resurfacing — not a new company write-up.
- Snap's Dec-2025 HTE framework (arXiv 2512.03060) is a real industrial paper on "users' journey" but targets ad influenceability/sensitivity, not retention-per-interaction credit — deliberately excluded from Candidates.
- Kuaishou's 2025–2026 OneRec-V2/OneMall work optimizes "App Stay Time" via unified ranking but makes no explicit per-interaction attribution claim — excluded as ranking optimization, not attribution.
- Platforms confirmed to have published *nothing* on this topic after direct search: Tencent, ByteDance (company-level, beyond the already-known RLUR lineage), LinkedIn, Duolingo, Roblox, Zynga, King, Supercell, EA, Riot, DoorDash (beyond pure A/B methodology), Lyft, Booking, Instacart, and every named dating app other than the two unnamed-platform academic papers (Candidates #8, #9).
- The two strongest hits overall (Candidates #8 and #9) are academic papers using real production dating/job-matching platform data with online experiments — they are the closest published analogs to the consumer's exact N7/swipe-attribution problem, but neither names its company.
- Two leads were found but not confirmed due to WebFetch 403 blocks (Netflix "Round 2" post, Airbnb "Future Value" post) — content is based on WebSearch snippets only; recommend a manual re-check if these become load-bearing.
- Not fetched for confirmation due to budget: a September 2025 Instagram "New Ranking Framework for Better Notification Quality" follow-up post (engineering.fb.com) — possible additional direct candidate, flagged for follow-up.
- "Attribution" in most generic web search results (row 14) refers to ad/marketing multi-touch attribution, not in-app interaction retention credit — this is a persistent false-positive pattern worth noting for future searches.
- PrefRec (arXiv 2212.02779, "Recommender Systems with Human Preferences for Reinforcing Long-term User Engagement") surfaced under ByteDance search but its industry affiliation/production-deployment status was not verified — omitted from Candidates pending confirmation.
